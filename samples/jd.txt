Business Analyst — Growth & Insights (Junior)
Company: ShopLane (e-commerce marketplace), Bengaluru

About the role
We're looking for a Business Analyst to partner with our Growth and Category teams. You'll own reporting for key funnel metrics, run deep-dive analyses, and translate business questions into data-driven recommendations.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dashboards (Power BI) tracking conversion funnel, retention and GMV.
- Write complex SQL (joins, window functions, CTEs) against our Snowflake warehouse.
- Run root-cause analyses when metrics move and present findings to senior stakeholders.
- Design and analyse A/B tests with the product team.
- Gather requirements and write clear documentation for new reports.

Requirements
- 0–2 years of experience in an analytics or BA role.
- Strong SQL and Excel; Power BI or Tableau experience.
- Understanding of e-commerce metrics (conversion, AOV, retention, cohort analysis).
- Basic statistics and A/B testing knowledge.
- Excellent communication; able to structure ambiguous problems.
- Nice to have: Python, Snowflake, experience with experimentation platforms.
